# Collaborative Exploration

This context describes people building a shared understanding of a topic within bounded collaborative spaces. The glossary names product concepts independently of their technical implementation.

## Language

**Workspace**:
The collaboration boundary in which Members conduct Explorations.
_Avoid_: Project, tenant

**Member**:
A person participating in a Workspace. Membership describes the person's relationship to that Workspace, not their global identity.
_Avoid_: User, account

**Role**:
A Member's authority within a Workspace.
_Avoid_: User type, access level

**Exploration**:
A shared effort to understand a topic by collecting and relating contributions from Members.
_Avoid_: Project, board, canvas
